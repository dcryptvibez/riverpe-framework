What changed between v2 and v4

v4 is a correction, not a restatement. Four findings changed the recommendations:

  • Settlement model. UEMOA Règlement n°06/2024/CM/UEMOA and Instruction n°01/07/2025/RFE art. 26 bar payment and e-money institutions from originating payments abroad. An approved intermediary bank is therefore mandatory, not optional.
  • Float. The minimum operating float rises from $50,000 to about $57,000, because the weekend settlement gap is three days, not two.
  • Unit economics. The funding-side collection fee was missing. Net margin falls from 1.58% to 1.24%, and break-even rises from $1.14m to $1,451,613 per month.
  • Market order. Brazil scores higher than Côte d'Ivoire on operability and still is not first. Scores measure operability; launch order is decided by access and whether the float can be funded. The DRC is excluded from scoring altogether.
